# V1 Test Dataset — Customer Support Scenarios

## Purpose

This dataset is used to test Prompt V1 against different types of customer support situations.

The scenarios are designed to test whether the AI can provide clear, helpful, professional, and safe responses.

---

## Test Case 01 — Late Delivery

**Customer Message:**

"My order was supposed to arrive yesterday, but I still haven't received it. What should I do?"

**Expected Behaviour:**

The AI should acknowledge the delayed delivery, provide helpful next steps, and request the order number if necessary.

---

## Test Case 02 — Refund Request

**Customer Message:**

"I want a refund for my order. How can I request one?"

**Expected Behaviour:**

The AI should explain that the refund process depends on the company's policy and ask for relevant order information.

---

## Test Case 03 — Damaged Product

**Customer Message:**

"My package arrived today, but the product inside is damaged. What can I do?"

**Expected Behaviour:**

The AI should apologise, explain possible next steps, and request relevant information or evidence if required.

---

## Test Case 04 — Wrong Product

**Customer Message:**

"I ordered a black shirt but received a white one. How can I get the correct item?"

**Expected Behaviour:**

The AI should acknowledge the mistake and explain the possible replacement or exchange process.

---

## Test Case 05 — Cancel Order

**Customer Message:**

"I just placed an order but I changed my mind. Can I cancel it?"

**Expected Behaviour:**

The AI should explain that cancellation may depend on whether the order has already been processed or shipped.

---

## Test Case 06 — Missing Information

**Customer Message:**

"Can you check my order?"

**Expected Behaviour:**

The AI should ask for information such as an order number because the request does not contain enough information.

---

## Test Case 07 — Angry Customer

**Customer Message:**

"This is ridiculous! I've been waiting for my order for two weeks and nobody is helping me!"

**Expected Behaviour:**

The AI should remain calm and professional, acknowledge the customer's frustration, and provide practical next steps.

---

## Test Case 08 — Unknown Question

**Customer Message:**

"Do you sell products that are available only in other countries?"

**Expected Behaviour:**

The AI should not invent company policies. It should explain that it needs more information or that it cannot confirm the policy.

---

## Test Case 09 — Unclear Request

**Customer Message:**

"I need help with my account."

**Expected Behaviour:**

The AI should ask a clear follow-up question to understand what account problem the customer is experiencing.

---

## Test Case 10 — Simple Question

**Customer Message:**

"What are your customer support hours?"

**Expected Behaviour:**

The AI should provide the hours if they are known. If the information is not available, it should clearly say that it cannot confirm the hours rather than inventing them.
